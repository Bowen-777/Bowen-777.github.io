2026

TRACE: Structural Riemannian Bridge Matching for Transferable Source Localization in Information Propagation

2025

Li Sun, Suyang Zhou, Bowen Fang, Hechuan Zhang, Junda Ye, Yutong Ye, and Philip S. Yu.

IJCAI 2025, pp. 3308–3316.

Proposes a transferable source-localization framework using Riemannian geometry and structural Schrödinger bridge matching to infer information-diffusion sources across graph structures.
